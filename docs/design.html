Design guidelines

How Dashi looks and moves

Every value here is read from the code: @dashi/ui's theme, the shell's theme.ts, and the compositor's renderer. Change one, and change it here in the same commit.

Principles

Calm by default

Nothing moves, flashes, or asks for attention unless the person needs it now. Quiet surfaces, one accent, gentle motion.

Content first, chrome recedes

Glass and hairlines instead of heavy frames. Toolbars merge into windows; the menu bar and Dock let the wallpaper through.

Familiar, then delightful

Start from what people know from macOS and Windows, so nothing has to be learned. Then add the touches that make it Dashi's: the Shapes, the ingredients, Kombu.

One color, one job

Kombu means "yours, selected"; ume means "close or destroy"; broth means "set aside or careful"; shiso means "an agent is acting". Never reuse a color for a second meaning.

Everything from the same tokens

Shell, apps, the login screen, and GTK apps all draw from one palette and one set of measures. Rust draws chrome from the same values the shell sends; no surface invents its own.

Motion explains

A window flies to the Dock tile it went to; a Space slides the way it lies. Motion shows where things went, quickly, and stops entirely with Reduce Motion.

Color

Two sets, light and dark, that fill the same names (packages/dashi-ui/src/theme/palette.ts). Translucent values are glass: they assume a wallpaper or window behind. Select a swatch to copy its value.

Translucent swatches show a checkerboard through them.

Typography

Inter everywhere (INTERFACE_FAMILY, appearance/look.ts), with Noto Sans for scripts Inter lacks and Noto Sans Mono for code. GTK apps get "Inter 11". The compositor's window titles are SemiBold 13. Every specimen below is editable.

  • Weights: Regular for reading, SemiBold (600) for labels that head something, Bold (700) for titles and the menu bar's app name. No Light, except the boot splash's title.
  • Muted text is the ink at 60% (textMuted), not a separate gray, so it works on any glass.

Shadows

Two, from mirin Metal's presets: sm (0 1 3 0 and 0 1 2 −1, both 10%) for controls and cards, xl (0 20 25 −5 and 0 8 10 −6, both 10%) for sheets and menus. Windows don't cast a shadow yet open.

Window chrome

Dashi's window controls are the Shapes: a circle closes, a bar minimizes, a rounded square zooms. Each shape says what it does even without color, and the colors are the ingredients. Hover over them, and switch the window's state.

  • Controls are vertically centered in the bar; a press within 8 px of one counts; under the pointer the group lights and the hovered control grows 1.18×.
  • Unfocused windows show gray controls, lit again on hover.
  • Standard bar 28 with a centered SemiBold 13 title, clear of 84 px each side. A unified window gives the app a 38 px toolbar strip and keeps a 76 × 38 corner for the controls.
  • An app with no toolbar controls (Terminal) takes the standard bar, not a unified one, and keeps it current with the window title (setTitle): one slim line, "dashi@dashi:~ — 96×26".
  • The zoom control uses a lighter kombu (#4A9A7E) than the accent so an 11 px square reads on the bar.
  • Windows resize from any edge: 6 px outside and 3 px inside the frame.

  • Nothing longer than 300 ms on the way to something the person asked for.
  • No springs, bounces, or overshoot in system UI, with one exception: the Dock's icons settle with a ~2 px spring as it comes in, while the panel is already in place and every icon already takes a press. Kombu's idle bob is the only loop, and only where Kombu appears.
  • Reduce Motion turns the flights, slides, and the Dock's entrance off; things simply appear where they go.
  • Windows open and close without animation today open.

Icons

App icons share one recipe (packages/shell/icons/): a 44 tile on a 48 grid, radius 10.5, a top-to-bottom gradient lighter on top, an optional white inner line, and a flat near-white glyph with round caps. Build one:

Glyphs sit in the middle 30 of the 48, drawn at 1.3–2 stroke on their own 16-unit grid. One idea per icon, no text, no perspective.

The set

Menu bar extras

Programs' own items sit left of the status glyphs (src/extras/; tray icons and applets, docs/applets.md), newest leftmost. They draw like the system's own so the bar reads as one:

  • 16 px pictures in 22 tall pills, 7 of padding each side; a label is 12 Regular, 5 from its picture. A pill whose menu is open takes the selection wash, as the bar's titles do.
  • Applets' glyphs and a tray icon's symbolic icon are templates, drawn in the bar's ink in Light and Dark Mode. A tray icon that only has a full-color picture keeps its colors.
  • Attention is a 6 px dot in the critical color at the pill's top right, never a flash.
  • A click activates or opens the menu; a right-click opens the menu, right-aligned under the item. On a narrow screen the extras give way from the left; the status glyphs and the clock never shrink.
  • The battery has its own menu: the charge, the power source, the time to full or empty, the draw in watts, the apps using significant energy (each opens Activity Monitor), and Battery Settings. Plugged in, it shows a bolt while charging and a plug while it holds its charge, and the HUD says “Charging · 54%” for 1.5 s.

Keyboard

❖ is Dashi's command key (the Super key), in the role ⌘ has on a Mac. Shortcuts are written in Mac order, ⌃ ⌥ ⇧ ❖ then the key, and drawn with @dashi/ui's Shortcut glyphs, never typed: fonts don't have ❖.

Wallpapers

Drawn from formulas, not photographs (packages/shell/wallpapers/generate.ts): 3840 × 2160 with a 2 × 2 Bayer dither, about 1.5 MB each. Dusk is the default and matches the compositor's built-in gradient, so a fresh session never flashes.

  • Live wallpapers are shaders: Northern Lights, Drift, and Tide, drawn at 320 × 180 and scaled up, at 15, 30, or 60 fps.
  • Video wallpapers loop muted, pause on battery by default, and leave the still picture for the lock screen.
  • New wallpapers are generated too, and keep the dusk family's quiet: soft gradients, no subjects, no text.

Open and close animationWindows appear and vanish.
Accent choicesKombu only. More accents would each need an ingredient and to stay clear of ume, broth, and shiso.
