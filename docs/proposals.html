Windows, and a roast

Two things. First, the window fixes asked for on 2026-11-05: the green control fills the screen instead of going full screen, tiling gets top and bottom halves and a shortcut map that holds together, and windows get depth so one can be told from the next. Second, every Dashi app was roasted by its own reviewer this morning, and what they found is gathered here into themes, per-app fixes and an order of work.

◆

Nothing here is picked yet. Cards outlined in green are the recommendation; "Today" marks what Dashi does now. Bugs cite path:line from release/0.4.0 at 74d917d; two were confirmed in the VM. The roast's full reports are in docs/roast/. Picks to make are at the end.

Found in the tiling code

How it works today: the shell's Arranger (packages/shell/src/arrange.ts) asks the compositor to frame a window with remember: true; the compositor keeps the frame it had as restore and gives it back on restore (crates/dashi-comp/src/wm/actions.rs:187-219). Tiles are left, right, four quarters, fill and restore (layout.ts:32); there is no top or bottom half. The green control asks for full screen and, with ⌥, zoom; a double-click on the title bar zooms (input/decorations.rs:96-117).

1 · Zoom on a tiled window shrinks it back instead of filling.The compositor reports zoomed whenever it remembers a frame (wm/windows.rs:283), and a tile remembers one; Arranger.zoom restores any window that says zoomed (arrange.ts:143). So ⌥-green, double-click and Window › Zoom on a left-tiled window put it back to its old size. Confirmed in the VM: Files tiled left reports zoomed: true.
2 · Moving or resizing a tiled window keeps a stale "back" frame.Only a drop in a drag zone touches restore (wm/zones.rs:107-117); the person's own move and resize (wm/grabs/move_grab.rs, resize_grab.rs) never clear it. A window dragged out of a tile keeps the tile's size, still says zoomed, and Restore (⌃❖↓) later jumps it to a frame from before the tile.
3 · Two different Fills.Tile fill (⌃❖↑, the menu-bar drag zone, Window › Fill) is the usable area less 6 px gaps and doesn't tell the app (layout.ts:65-66, arrange.ts:135); Zoom is the whole usable area and sets xdg Maximized (layout.ts:70-71, arrange.ts:145). The Window menu lists both, "Zoom" and "Fill", side by side (menus/model.ts:206-209). Apps that draw their own frame keep their rounded corners and resize borders after Fill because nobody told them. Confirmed in the VM: Fill gives x 6, width 1634 on a 1646-point screen.
4 · The green control's meaning is decided in Rust.A plain press emits fullscreen on and ⌥ emits zoom (input/decorations.rs:96-104). Which action a control means is policy, and the file's own header says the shell decides (lines 2-4). It should report the press and its modifiers and let arrange.ts choose.
5 · Restore with nothing to restore does nothing, silently.restore_window is a no-op without a remembered frame (wm/actions.rs:208-219), and Window › Restore is enabled for every window (menus/model.ts:174-179). It should be disabled unless the window is arranged.
6 · Pressing a tile again does nothing; there's no way to another screen.A second ⌃❖← recomputes the same frame on the window's own screen (arrange.ts:129-136). With two screens, the keyboard can't move a window to the other one.
7 · ⌃❖↑ and ⌃❖↓ are spent on Fill and Restore.That is why top and bottom halves have no keys (keymap.ts:91-94). The arrows read as directions everywhere else in the map, so ↑ meaning "everything" and ↓ meaning "undo" is the odd one out.

Not bugs, but worth keeping in mind: the agent tool tile_window takes its choices from TILES (agents/tools/windows.ts:97), so new tiles reach agents for free; and its description promises "zoomed (tiled or filling the screen)", which changes meaning once bug 1 is fixed.

Today a click on the green square goes full screen: the menu bar and Dock leave, and getting back needs the pointer at the top edge or ⌃❖F. That is the Mac's default. Most of the time the person wanted a big window, not a different mode.

One Fill
  • Fill replaces both Zoom and tile fill: the whole usable area with no gap, and the app is told it is maximized (fixes bug 3). The Window menu says "Fill" once; "Zoom" goes.
  • The shell remembers what it did. Arranger keeps each window's arrangement (fill, a tile, or none) and reports it to the menus and the agent tool; the compositor's zoomed flag stops standing in for it (fixes bug 1).
  • Dragging a filled or tiled window gives it its own size back under the pointer, as on every other desktop, and forgets the arrangement; a person's resize forgets it too (fixes bug 2). The compositor reports the grab's start; the shell decides.
  • The compositor reports the press: window-control {window, control: "zoom", alt, hold}, and the shell picks Fill, Full Screen or the menu (fixes bug 4). docs/compositor-ipc.md changes in the same commit.
  • Back (Window › Back to Previous Size) is disabled when there is nothing to go back to (fixes bug 5).

Depth between windows

Windows cast no shadow today (the design guidelines say so), so two light windows that overlap read as one sheet of paper, and in dark mode a window melts into the one behind it. The compositor already draws each window's rounded corners with a shader (crates/dashi-comp/src/render/rounded.rs). How the compositor draws it
  • The shell sends the look, the compositor draws it. A new decor request carries shadow {focused, other} (offset, blur, alpha), edge {light, dark} and ring {on, color, width}; values live in @dashi/ui's tokens and in design.html. Starting values: focused 0 18 40 −8 at 38% plus 0 2 6 at 16%; others 0 8 20 −6 at 22%; dark mode raises alphas by about half.
  • The shadow is one quad per window behind its surface, drawn with a closed-form blurred rounded-rectangle shader next to rounded.rs, so there is no blur pass or offscreen buffer. The hairline is a parameter of the existing corner shader. The ring is drawn outside the frame.
  • Focus changes cross-fade the two shadow strengths over 150 ms (ease-out cubic, as every compositor motion); Reduce Motion makes it instant.
Edge cases
  • Full screen: no shadow, edge or ring; direct scanout keeps working.
  • Fill: the edge and ring stay, the shadow goes (it would only fall on the menu bar and Dock).
  • Tiles 6 px apart: shadows are clipped to the window's own screen and, for tiled windows, drawn short (offset 0, blur 8) so they shade the gap, not the neighbor.
  • Apps that draw their own frame (Xwayland, client-side decorations): the shadow follows the window's geometry, not its buffer; a client whose buffer is bigger than its geometry already draws a shadow, and gets none from Dashi.
  • Sheets and popups: sheets get the xl shadow from the same shader (they have none today, a known 0.4.0 issue); app menus (xdg popups) get the menu shadow; the menu bar, Dock and other layer surfaces get none.
  • Overview and the switcher: thumbnails get a scaled shadow so they lift off the wallpaper; the hover highlight stays Overview's own.
  • Screenshots: ⇧❖4 then Space captures the window without its shadow; agents' captures are unchanged.
  • Damage: the shadow's rectangle joins the window's damage when it moves, resizes or changes focus (two windows per focus change); nothing else redraws. Check frame time in the VM with ten overlapping windows before shipping.

Every claim in them cites code or a screenshot. What follows is the merge.

Order of work

Wave 1: remove the embarrassment

Small, mostly app and token changes; each is a day or less, and together they change the first impression.

  • The window fixes on this page: Fill on the green control, the new chords, bugs 1–7. shell compositor
  • AppWindow without subtitle and footer; contrast and job tokens (ume, broth, moss; darker accent fill). @dashi/ui
  • Confirm before deleting passwords and cards, Revert, closing a running tab; Enter never grants. @dashi/ui
  • Applet Store opens on Installed without a key; Components unpinned from the default Dock.
  • Terminal palette, memo fix, ⇧❖W; Editor caret jump, ❖W asks, window titles.
  • Calculator locale, paste and spacing; Activity Monitor numbers, one row, sampling in a worker.
  • Settings' two Dark Mode controls, the sidebar colors; Calendar's hidden-calendar trap and palette.
  • Portal chooser Back and Up, real double-click, the Copies field; Software's local recommended list; Preview's empty state; Notes' conflict copy.

Wave 2: the platform pieces

Each unlocks several apps at once. Most of these are in mirin or @dashi/ui, not in an app.

  • mirin TextInput caret placement and selection, then a document view; modifiers on onPress; tooltips; later the accessibility tree.
  • @dashi/ui KeySink on onKeyDown and a focus model; the missing controls; PopupMenu with separators, keys and clamping; one alert frame; a token lint.
  • kit a shared undo stack; a window-visibility signal; Components as the gallery.
  • compositor shadows, the hairline and the optional ring; sheets' shadow.
  • Settings the settings registry behind search; one permission language and a Privacy & Security pane.

Wave 3: the big ideas

  • One file browser everywhere (Files, the portal, dashi-dialog, Dashi's apps).
  • Settings as addressable entries, with "Recently changed" and Undo, agents included.
  • Software as the trust card, with applets folded in.
  • Activity Monitor app-first, sharing one cost model with the battery menu and Force Quit.
  • Calendar's grid as the editor; Notes as one live page; Editor's document surface with autosave.
  • Terminal with shell integration; Preview's strip of pages; Calculator's expression line.
