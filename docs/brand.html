Brand guidelines

A quiet base, made with care

Dashi is the stock Japanese cooking starts from: kombu and bonito simmered into something you barely notice and everything depends on. The OS aims for the same: a calm foundation that makes everything on it taste better.

The name

Say it

DAH-shee

出汁 · Japanese soup stock

Write it

Dashi: one word, capital D. In system UI and docs it is just "Dashi", never "the Dashi".

Not DASHI, DashiOS, Dashi OS, or dashi (except in code, package, and command names: dashi-comp, ID=dashi). dashiOS is only the repository's name.

Inspired, not imitated

Dashi takes what people already love about macOS and Windows and makes it its own. The test for every borrowed idea: would someone who knows both feel at home, and would nobody mistake it for either?

⚠︎

Never ship another company's assets. No Apple or Microsoft icons, glyphs (⌘, ⊞), fonts (SF, Segoe), wallpapers, sounds, or product names in the UI. Reinterpret the idea, then draw it fresh.

The mark

A bowl and three wisps of steam: dashi, freshly made. The app tile sets it in white on a kombu gradient; the glyph alone works wherever a single color is needed.

Construction

  • Tile: 44 × 44 on a 48 grid, corner radius 10.5, filled top to bottom from #5FAE95 to #2F6F5A, with a 1.5 white inner line at 18%: the same recipe as every Dashi app icon (Icons).
  • Glyph: drawn on a 16-unit grid: the bowl a half-ellipse 13 wide, three wisps 3 apart, 1.3 strokes with round caps. Near-white #F4F4F6 on the tile.
  • Clear space: an eighth of the tile on every side (the dashed line). Nothing else enters it.
  • Smallest size: 16 px for the tile, 12 px for the glyph. Below that the wisps merge.
  • Files: packages/shell/icons/setup.svg (tile), spin/dashi-logos/ (PNG and Plymouth frames).

Lockup

Dashi

The wordmark is Inter Bold at −0.02em tracking, its cap height about half the tile's. The gap between them is a quarter of the tile. Use the lockup where the name isn't already nearby; inside the OS, the mark alone is enough.

Don't

Ingredients

Dashi's colors are named after what goes into the pot. Each has one job; select one to copy its hex. Full tokens are on the design page.

Neutrals: ink #1D1D1F and paper #F5F5F7, swapped in Dark Mode. Success uses moss #4F9A3E, a green deliberately not the accent. New colors get an ingredient's name and one job, or they don't get in.

Voice

Dashi speaks like a good host: calm, plain, and brief. It tells you what will happen, not how it works, and it never shouts.

Calm

No exclamation marks in system UI. No urgency it doesn't have. Errors say what happened and what to do next.

Plain

Everyday words over jargon: "Window corners", not "Decoration radius". Name things for what people see.

Brief

One sentence where one will do. Details under a label are full sentences, with a period.

Kind

Never blame the person. Ask before anything that can't be undone, and say exactly what will be lost.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for labels, settings, buttons, and headings: "Accent color", "Reduce motion". Names keep their capitals: "Skip Setup", "Open System Settings".
  • Title Case for menu bar commands, as on the Mac: "New Calendar…", "Move to Trash…".
  • An ellipsis (…) when a command needs more before it acts: it opens a window, asks a question, or confirms. A real ellipsis character, not three dots.
  • Shortcuts are written with glyphs in Mac order: ⌃ ⌥ ⇧ ❖ then the key (⇧❖3). ❖ is drawn as an SVG (@dashi/ui's Shortcut), never typed into text: fonts don't have it.
  • "Dashi", not "we", in system UI. The docs may say "we".
