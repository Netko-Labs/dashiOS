# System Settings (and Setup Assistant)

Paths are relative to `packages/shell/src/` unless they start with `dashi-ui/` (that is `packages/dashi-ui/src/`). Screenshots: `settings.png`, `settings-appearance.png`, `settings-battery.png`, `settings-passwords.png`, `settings-agents.png`, `settings-desktop-dock.png`, `setup.png`.

**The roast**

- Forgetting a Wi-Fi network asks first. Deleting a saved password doesn't: it takes one click on a bright red button. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth get a "Forget… → Cancel / Forget" step (`panes/network.tsx:110-116`, `panes/bluetooth.tsx:322-345`). Passwords and payment cards only get a filled red "Delete" on every row, with no confirmation, no undo and no error handling (`panes/passwords.tsx:120,191`, `panes/payments.tsx:164`).
- The sidebar is Apple's color picker with the labels filed off: `#0a84ff`, `#30d158`, `#ff453a`, `#5e5ce6` and `#a2845e` are copied straight into `settings-app/panes.ts:47-66`. As a result, Appearance wears the agent purple and Notifications wears the "destroy" red (settings.png). The brand rule is "kombu instead of blue; ingredients instead of system colors", and that rule has been ignored here.
- There's no search box. In 2026, a System Settings with 20 panes, a Battery page longer than the window, and a Network page that sends you to `nm-connection-editor or nmcli` (`panes/network.tsx:189`) has no search. The only search field in the whole app is for keyboard layouts (`panes/keyboard.tsx:65-73`).
- Appearance gives you two controls for Dark Mode, and they disagree. The Light/Dark previews clear Auto (`panes/appearance.tsx:89-92`); the Dark Mode switch right under them doesn't (`:127`). With Auto on, flipping the switch turns Dark Mode on while the preview still says Auto, and the schedule flips it back at 7.
- Desktop & Dock copies macOS word for word: "When switching to an application, switch to a Space with open windows for the application" runs to two lines, and the caption names "Mission Control" (`panes/desktop-dock.tsx:206,213`, settings-desktop-dock.png). "Inspired, not imitated" gets to the second sentence and gives up.
- The Agents pane is the heart of an agent-first OS, and it's a card with six identical lavender chips and one gray "Revoke". "Sign in for you" carries the same weight as "See notifications", and there's no way to take back just one of them (settings-agents.png, `panes/agents.tsx:220-252`).

### Every pane, briefly

- **Appearance**: The previews are nice, but the preview "wallpaper" is always kombu green, not your actual wallpaper (`appearance.tsx:28,59`). The Dark Mode switch duplicates the previews and disagrees with them, and "Accent color: Kombu" is a setting with one possible value. Auto's 7 PM/7 AM schedule is hard-coded and only explained in a footnote.
- **Wallpaper**: A row labeled "Add Photo…" with a button labeled "Add Photo…" (`wallpaper.tsx:90-91`). The fallback when there's no file chooser is "Type the path of a photo" (`:76,94`), which is a developer's escape hatch shown to everyone.
- **Displays**: This one is solid: it reverts a mode change you don't keep (`displays.tsx:239-240`) and has drag arrangement. But it's also where Night Shift, X11 scaling and panel overdrive live (`:242-244`), so "Displays" covers four topics, and Night Shift isn't findable by search.
- **Desktop & Dock**: Covered in depth below. It's thin, too: no Dock size or position, and no hot corners.
- **Network**: Wi-Fi rows, VPN switches, and Forget with a confirm, which is the model the rest of Settings should follow. But known networks are listed twice (in the scan list and under "Known Networks", `network.tsx:88-89,101-120`). There's no address info for Wi-Fi, only for Ethernet, no "join other network", and the VPN empty state tells a normal person to open `nmcli`.
- **Bluetooth**: Good busy states and a confirm on Forget. A "Search again" row with a "Search" button sits in its own card (`bluetooth.tsx:396-402`). "Nearby Devices — Searching…" puts status into a heading.
- **Sound**: Has a live input meter, which is a nice touch. Mute is a separate row with a switch instead of a button next to the slider, so each side takes two rows (`sound.tsx:94-96`). The slider has no keyboard control.
- **Battery & Power**: Covered in depth below.
- **Graphics**: Shown only when there's a second GPU (`view.tsx:247-255`), so its "This computer has one graphics processor." empty state can never be seen (`graphics.tsx:23-29`). "Restart to apply" is a row whose only content is a button.
- **Keyboard**: The layout picker has its own search, the only search in the app. There's no shortcuts list and no way to have more than one layout; that's left to "(several layouts set in settings.json)" (`keyboard.tsx:64`).
- **Trackpad**: The clearest pane. "Ignore the pad while typing" is exactly the right copy. No gestures, even though search promises them.
- **Mouse**: Fine. "Adaptive / Flat" is jargon, but at least it's explained.
- **Date & Time**: The live "The menu bar reads: …" preview is lovely. There's no manual time entry, and the NTP switch still looks clickable when nothing will happen (`date-time.tsx:183`).
- **Language & Region**: It honestly says "Dashi's own menus are English for now". Errors are hand-drawn critical text instead of `Failure`.
- **Lock Screen**: Two rows: a setting copied from Battery, and a "Lock Screen" row whose button is also labeled "Lock Screen" (`lock-screen.tsx:45-46`). No password delay and no message on the lock screen.
- **Users & Accounts**: The picture, the name and a password sheet that agents can't see, which is a good privacy detail. There's only one user, and the footnote is about GDM, SDDM and greetd (`users.tsx:316`).
- **Notifications**: One switch plus a caption saying it's the same switch as in Control Center (`notifications.tsx:7-18`). That's a placeholder, not a pane.
- **Passwords**: Covered in depth below. Note that the screenshot shows only a flat dark slab: the agent's capture is masked while this pane is open (`agents/secrets/vault-view.ts:23`, `agents/mask.ts:114`). That's a good privacy decision, but it means this pane was reviewed from code only.
- **Agents**: Covered in depth below.
- **About**: Clean. "Name" is read-only, nothing can be copied, and "Setup Assistant · Open…" is the only action.

### The five worst, in depth

**1. Passwords (with Payments inside it).** This is where a person's most valuable data lives, and it gets the least care. From top to bottom it shows: two source switches; a "Save new logins to" choice; a caption about agents; a hand-drawn "Logins in Dashi" heading (`passwords.tsx:181`) that doesn't match the `Caption above` headings used everywhere else; a list where every row has an unlabeled "Ask | Never" pill pair next to a filled red "Delete" (`:107-124`); an always-open form of five rows, ending in a row with an empty label (`:100`); a caption; the vault status line, which may already have appeared in the empty list (`:184,200`); then a "Payments" heading with cards, a second always-open form, and a limits form that asks for your login password inline (`payments.tsx:74-84`). None of the deletes asks first, and none of the async calls handles errors (`passwords.tsx:190-191`, `payments.tsx:148,164`). A failed card load is reported as "No cards. Agents can't pay until you add one", which is a lie told kindly. Day-one basics are missing: search, reveal after authenticating, copy, edit, and a password generator. The vault's error text goes straight into the UI: "Dashi's vault isn't running (ECONNREFUSED …)." (`:134`).

**2. Agents.** In an OS whose whole idea is "you decide what agents may do", this pane is a read-only list with one button. Problems: (a) The scopes are chips, not switches, so trimming one permission means revoking everything and waiting to be asked again. (b) There's no risk hierarchy. "Sign in for you" (the agent can use your passwords) and "Manage windows and apps" look the same as "See notifications" (settings-agents.png). (c) There's no live state. The menu bar pill says "Remote agent · Taking a screenshot" while the pane shows nothing about it being connected right now. (d) There's no last-used time, no history, and no list of agents you denied or allowed once. (e) There's no master "Let agents connect" switch; the only kill switch is the ⌃❖Esc chord that Setup mentions (`setup/pages/agents.tsx:69`). (f) The title is the raw ssh key, "dashi-vm-agent" (`agent-names.ts:295`). (g) The row is a custom View with 12 px padding all round instead of `Row`'s 8×12 (`agents.tsx:233`), so it doesn't line up with other panes. (h) "Revoke" is a neutral button. Revoking shouldn't need a confirmation (speed matters), but the pane should say what just happened, for example "Revoked. dashi-vm-agent was disconnected.", and offer Undo for a few seconds.

**3. Appearance.** This is the first pane anyone opens (it's `FIRST_PANE`, `panes.ts:69`), and it gets the most basic rule wrong: one setting, one control. The Light/Dark/Auto previews *are* the Dark Mode setting. The "Dark Mode" switch under them repeats it, and its handler doesn't clear `auto` (`appearance.tsx:127` vs `:90`). That lets the screen show "Auto" selected while the switch is on, and then the schedule reverts it. "Accent color" is presented as a row but is just a static label (`:129-137`). The previews draw a fixed kombu "wallpaper" (`:28,59`) instead of a thumbnail of your own, so they don't preview *your* desktop. Auto's 7 PM/7 AM timing is hard-coded and explained only in a footnote (`:157`), with no sunset option and no custom times. Night Shift, which in Dashi does have a schedule, is a pane away in Displays.

**4. Battery & Power.** The pane is good engineering and confusing to use. On the screenshot's machine it opens on Power Mode with "Balanced, chosen by you." (settings-battery.png), then Battery Health, Processor, Sleep, and Lid and Power Button (`battery.tsx:50-150`). The charge-limit value sits alone on a line under the slider and doesn't move while you drag (`battery-laptop.tsx:220-226`). "Charge Now" is disabled at 100% with no reason given (`:232`). "Full by a time" offers only 06:00–09:00 (`:249`). Processor exposes the energy performance preference (EPP) as "Prefer: By mode / Battery / Balance / Speed" (`:273-278`), a choice most people can't make sensibly. The lid section shows three rows that look like settings but are read-only logind values, with `logind.conf` named in the caption (`battery.tsx:131-147`). "Turn display off after" appears here and in Lock Screen. And the mode copy is written twice with different keys (`battery.tsx:31`, `battery-laptop.tsx:175`).

**5. Desktop & Dock.** It's short, and almost everything in it needs fixing. "Show the Dock" comes *after* "Automatically hide and show", and turning it off leaves the autohide switch fully active (`desktop-dock.tsx:193-201`). The Spaces row is macOS's own sentence, wrapped to two lines (settings-desktop-dock.png). The help text "Turned off, there is no Dock at all." / "Turned off, the window comes to the Space you are on instead." uses an awkward dangling "Turned off," construction twice. The caption names an Apple product (Mission Control) and types its shortcuts into the text (`:213`), while the caption above draws them correctly with `keys` (`:203`). And for a pane named after the Dock, it has no Dock size, no position (left/bottom/right), and no "show open apps only".

### Settings window, sidebar and header

- **Header strip** (settings-agents.png). The title sits in the strip the window controls are centered on (`view.tsx:234-243`), with no background, hairline or scroll edge of its own; the strip and the content are both `#F5F5F7` (measured). That keeps to the one-chrome-row rule, which is good. But (a) the title is 14 Bold instead of the spec's 13; (b) once the content scrolls, the groups disappear under an invisible line, so the strip should get a hairline only when scrolled; (c) the rest of the strip is empty, and that's where search (right-aligned) and Back/Forward (left of the title) belong without adding a row.
- **The save-error bar** (`view.tsx:271-276`) is a second bar along the bottom of the window, showing raw error text cut to one line. Move it into the pane as a `Failure`.
- **Sidebar.** There's no search, a 7-item mixed group, Apple-hex tiles, one glyph for two panes, and no badges (an update, or a Wi-Fi network that needs signing in, would naturally show here; `SidebarItem` supports `badge` at `dashi-ui/sidebar.tsx:51`, but nothing uses it).

### Setup Assistant

Code: `setup/` (`start.tsx`, `flow.ts`, `frame.tsx`, `view.tsx`, `pages/*`), app id `dev.dashi.setup` (`apps.ts:136`). Screenshot: setup.png.

- **What works.** Every page changes the real settings directly (`setup/view.tsx:1-5`), so Back never "loses" anything. The Wi-Fi page is skipped on a wired machine (`flow.ts:58`). The Agents page is honest and well written about consent (`pages/agents.tsx:8-13`). The Done summary is a nice receipt.
- **Skipping is a trap.** On the Wi-Fi page the footer reads "Skip Setup … Back · Skip" (`frame.tsx:223`, `view.tsx:317-318`): two different skips, 400 px apart. "Skip Setup" ends the whole assistant with no confirmation (`start.tsx:149-152`). The close button means "skip" after 2 s (`start.tsx:168-170`), and the window has a zoom control it has no use for.
- **The welcome page** (setup.png) is a 700×640 window with a 96 px bowl, a title and two lines, then about 160 px of nothing above an 8-dot footer. On a live USB the most important action, "Install Dashi…", is a secondary button under the text (`pages/welcome.tsx:25-29`), while "Get Started" is the primary.
- **The dots aren't stable.** Picking extras inserts the Installing page, so the count goes from 8 to 9 partway through (`flow.ts:99`, `view.tsx:328-329`).
- **Copy and consistency.** Page titles are Title Case ("Time Zone", `flow.ts:34`), but the Done rows say "Time zone" (`done.tsx:164`). The Appearance page closes with trivia, "Accent color is Kombu, a green from Japanese cooking." (kombu is kelp; `pages/appearance.tsx:108`). Done reports a "12-hour clock" under Appearance, although setup never asked about it (`done.tsx:168`). The heading tiles reuse Settings' Apple-blue and orange values (`view.tsx:268-278`). Errors are inline critical text, the eighth error pattern (`pages/wifi.tsx:80`, `language.tsx:68`, `timezone.tsx:93`).
- **Keyboard.** Return doesn't Continue and Esc doesn't go Back (no key handling in `setup/`). A first-run assistant is exactly where someone might not have a working pointer yet. The menu bar offers File › New Window for it (`menus/model.ts:136`).
- **Agents page length.** It has four promise paragraphs plus every scope with its detail (`pages/agents.tsx:8-50`), more text than all the other pages together, and no decision to make. Keep three short promises and put the scope list behind "What agents can ask for…".

**Improvements**

1. **Recommended.** *What:* Give every irreversible action one confirm pattern. Promote Network's inline "Forget… → Cancel / Forget" (`network.tsx:110-116`) to a `@dashi/ui` `ConfirmButton`, and use it for login and card Delete, Bluetooth Forget, and Skip Setup. Row deletes become quiet critical-ink text buttons, not filled red. Every async action gets a `.catch` that shows a `Failure`. *Why:* It's the brand's "Ask before anything that can't be undone", and today a password is one misclick away from gone. *Size:* S.
2. **Recommended.** *What:* Add search to the existing header strip: a field right-aligned, opened with ❖F, no new row. Back it with a static registry of every row (`{pane, rowId, label, detail, keywords}`). Typing replaces the sidebar list with matching *rows* ("Charge limit — Battery & Power"). Choosing one opens the pane, scrolls to the row and lights it with the selection wash for a second. The launcher uses the same registry instead of `PANE_KEYWORDS`. *Why:* It's the first thing people try in a settings app, and today half the panes can't be found. *Size:* M.
3. **Recommended.** *What:* Regroup and recolor the sidebar. Proposed groups: **You** (Users & Accounts, with your avatar), **Look** (Appearance, Wallpaper, Displays, Desktop & Dock), **Connections** (Network, Bluetooth, Sound), **Power** (Battery & Power, Graphics), **Input** (Keyboard, Trackpad, Mouse), **Time & language** (Date & Time, Language & Region), **Privacy & security** (Lock Screen, Passwords, Agents, Notifications), then About. For tiles, drop the Apple hexes. Use neutral graphite tiles with white glyphs, keep shiso for Agents only so it stands out by meaning, and give Passwords a key glyph. *Why:* One color, one job. Today red means "Sound" and purple means "Appearance". *Size:* S.
4. **Recommended.** *What:* Fix Appearance's controls: the previews are the only Dark Mode control (Control Center keeps its tile). Replace the footnote with an "Auto" detail row ("Sunset to sunrise" or custom times, shared with Night Shift's schedule). Hide "Accent color" until there's more than one. Draw the previews over a thumbnail of the current wallpaper. *Why:* This is the first pane people see, and it shows two controls for one setting that disagree with each other. *Size:* S.
5. **Recommended.** *What:* Rebuild Agents as a consent dashboard. Each agent is a row (friendly name, where it runs, "Connected now" in shiso, last used). Pressing it opens a detail view with every scope as a switch, ordered by risk: "Sign in for you", "Pay" and "Manage windows" first, with a one-line consequence each. Add a master switch, "Let agents connect", at the top. Revoke takes effect at once and shows an inline "Revoked · Undo" for 5 s. List agents allowed once, and denied ones, under "Asked before". *Why:* This is Dashi's main difference from other systems, and right now it's the plainest pane. *Size:* M.
6. *What:* Split Passwords. **Passwords** gets a searchable list. **One big idea**

**Make every setting an addressable object, and show who changed it.** Today Settings has 20 panes of hand-placed rows. Instead, keep one registry entry per setting (id, label, detail, keywords, control type, read/write, pane, risk) and draw the rows from it. That registry then drives search in the header, the launcher, deep links (`settings://battery/charge-limit`), the agent tools that already change settings, and a new **"Recently changed"** list at the top of the sidebar. Each change there shows its author and an Undo: "Dark Mode on · you · 2 min ago", or "Charge limit 80% · dashi-vm-agent · just now", in shiso. On an agent-first OS, being able to see and undo any change, including an agent's, is what nobody else has, and it fixes search, duplicates and consistency along the way, because a setting can live in only one place.

**Cross-app notes**

- **Controls belong in `@dashi/ui`, with keyboard support.** `Switch` lives in `shell/src/wifi/switch.tsx` and `Slider` in `settings-app/ui.tsx`. Neither they nor `Choices` or `Button` (`dashi-ui/controls.tsx:105-183`) can take focus. Add a focus ring token, Tab order, and Space/Return/arrow keys to the shared controls once.
- **Need a destructive-action pattern.** `Button danger` is a filled red primary (`controls.tsx:161`), which is too loud for a row action and has no confirm. Add a quiet critical-ink row button plus an inline confirm (the Network/Bluetooth pattern), and write the rule in design.html: "irreversible means confirm or undo, never neither".
- **Need one error component and a rule.** `Failure` (`shell/src/settings-app/panes/parts.tsx:137`) should move to `@dashi/ui`. Rule: under the group it's about, in critical ink, in plain words, never errno text, never shown as an empty state, never a bottom bar.
- **Sidebar tile palette.** The design doc has no rule for tile colors, so Settings and Setup copied Apple's (`settings-app/panes.ts:47-66`, `setup/view.tsx:268-278`). Define a tile set from ingredients and neutrals, reserve shiso for agents and ume for destroy, and say what a selected row does to its tile.
- **Shared building blocks are copied per app.** `Value` (3 copies), the two `ListRow`s (`parts.tsx:63`, `keyboard.tsx:20`), `MiniButton` (24 px) next to `Button` (28 px), and three hand-made text fields (`passwords.tsx:42`, `payments.tsx`, `users.tsx:231`). They should be `@dashi/ui` components, along with one section-heading component instead of a mix of `Caption above` and hand-made 13 SemiBold text.
- **Unified toolbar spec.** Settings draws its title at 14 Bold, while the spec says 13 (`view.tsx:238`). Define the scroll-edge behavior (a hairline appears once content scrolls under the strip) and what may sit in the strip (search, Back/Forward) so apps don't add second rows.
- **Brand lint.** Add a check for Apple product names in UI strings ("Mission Control", "Night Shift"; decide on "Control Center", "Spaces", "Setup Assistant") and for typed ⌃/❖ in text outside `keys`/`Shortcut`. Also lint Title Case on buttons: the brand asks for sentence case, but most of the code uses Title Case ("Change Password…", "Turn Off", "Save Login"). Either the rule or the code has to change.
- **Error boundaries.** `openOverlay` (`shell/src/overlay.tsx:39`) has none, and nothing else in the repo does. One at the overlay level would protect every in-shell window (Settings, Setup, Force Quit) from taking the shell's surface down.
- **Page width.** `Page` (`settings-app/ui.tsx:182`) and the app kit should cap the content width (about 680) and center it, so grouped rows stay readable in maximized windows.
